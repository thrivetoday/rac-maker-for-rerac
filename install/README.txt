RAC Maker 0.1.0 for ReRAC (test build)
Make your own Ratchet & Clank levels and play them in ReRAC.

BEFORE YOU START
  ReRAC must be set up: the ReRAC Launcher with Ratchet & Clank installed from your own
  disc image (NTSC-U). https://github.com/re-rac/rerac-launcher

INSTALL
  1. Unzip this whole folder.
  2. Double-click install.bat (it finds your ReRAC folder by itself and prepares the object
     pictures from your game, about 30 seconds).
  3. Start "RAC Maker" from your desktop.

  It adds RAC Maker as one more ReRAC version ("0.3.6 + RAC Maker"). Your normal ReRAC,
  your saves and your game data are not changed.
  Optional: "ReRAC Launcher with RAC Maker" in the Start menu is a preview of the launcher
  with a RAC Maker tile (map library, import, export).

REMOVE
  Run uninstall.bat. Your maps stay in Documents\RAC Maker.

USE
  F1 = help in the editor. F5 = play your level, F5 again = back to the editor.
  Share a level: main menu, pick a map, Share. Send the .racmap file.
  Open one you got: main menu, Import... (it finds .racmap files in Downloads and on the Desktop).

FEEDBACK
  ReRAC Discord, or https://github.com/thrivetoday/rac-maker-for-rerac/issues
  Crash logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\rerac\logs
